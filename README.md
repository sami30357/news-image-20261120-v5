# The Friday Edition — V5

Sum up 20 November's world news in one image. One page, 34 verified stories, 7 regions — a parchment broadsheet with a deep-green masthead, built to the standard of the day's best pages and different from all of them.

- **Bounty:** [Sum up 20 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120) — $250 in USDC, top 50 pay.
- **The page:** three drawn heroes (Kyiv, Cyclone Akvan, the AI sell-off), ten drawn mid stories, a seven-moment timeline, a six-number stat strip, a records strip, a map with numbered pins, 21 briefs, and a two-part date check. Every story carries a verification tag — CONFIRMED, OFFICIAL, REPORTED or CLAIM — explained in the footer legend.
- **Run:** `python3 render.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024). `node main.ts` copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
